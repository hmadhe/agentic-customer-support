# VoltCart Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened laptops, tablets and phones can be returned within 15 days of delivery.

## Condition of returned items

Returned items must include the original packaging, all accessories, cables and manuals. Items with missing parts may be refused or refunded only partially.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Software and digital downloads after the activation code has been revealed
- Gift cards
- In-ear headphones and earbuds that have been opened, for hygiene reasons
- Clearance items marked "Final sale"

## Restocking fee

Opened laptops, cameras and drones have a 15% restocking fee. There is no restocking fee if the item is defective or we sent the wrong item.

## How to start a return

Go to **Account > Orders**, select the order and choose **Return item**. We email you a prepaid return label. Return shipping is free if the item is defective or we made a mistake. Otherwise, $7.99 is deducted from your refund.

## Refunds

After we receive and inspect your return, we refund the original payment method within 5–7 business days. Original shipping costs are refunded only if the return is due to our mistake. We do not offer direct exchanges: return the item and place a new order.

## Damaged or wrong items

If your item arrives damaged, or we sent the wrong item, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos through **Account > Orders > Report a problem**. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping costs.
