# VoltCart Payments Policy

## Accepted payment methods

VoltCart accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and VoltCart gift cards.

## When you are charged

Your card is authorized when you place the order, and charged only when the order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, you are charged for each package when it ships.

## Paying in installments

Orders of $150 or more can be paid in installments with VoltCart Pay Later. You can choose 3, 6 or 12 monthly payments. Plans of 3 months are interest-free. Pay Later is subject to an approval check at checkout.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we hold the order for 48 hours and email you to update your payment details. If the payment is not updated within 48 hours, the order is cancelled.

## Gift cards

VoltCart gift cards never expire and cannot be exchanged for cash. You can use up to 3 gift cards on one order.

## Currency and taxes

Prices are shown in US dollars for the United States and Canadian dollars for Canada. Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address.
