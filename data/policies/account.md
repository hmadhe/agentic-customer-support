# VoltCart Account Policy

## Creating an account

You can check out as a guest, but you need a VoltCart account to start returns online, track all your orders in one place and manage Protection Plans.

## Passwords and sign-in

Use the **Forgot password** link on the sign-in page to reset your password. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 30 minutes.

## Two-factor authentication

You can turn on two-factor authentication under **Account > Security**, using an authenticator app or text message codes.

## Changing your details

You can change your email address, phone number and saved addresses under **Account > Settings**. You can change the delivery address of an order only within 1 hour of placing it, and never after it has shipped.

## Order history

Your order history is kept in your account for 3 years.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account, go to **Account > Privacy** and choose **Delete account**. Deletion is completed within 30 days. You cannot delete your account while you have an order that has not been delivered yet.

## Marketing emails

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or under **Account > Settings**. Order and shipping emails cannot be turned off.
