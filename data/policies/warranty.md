# VoltCart Warranty Policy

## Manufacturer warranty

All new products sold by VoltCart come with the manufacturer's warranty, usually 1 year from the date of delivery. The manufacturer handles these repairs. VoltCart support can help you find the right manufacturer contact.

## Refurbished products

Refurbished products come with a 90-day VoltCart warranty instead of a manufacturer warranty. If a refurbished product develops a fault within 90 days, we repair or replace it for free.

## VoltCart Protection Plan

The VoltCart Protection Plan extends coverage to 2 or 3 years and adds accidental damage protection, such as drops and liquid spills, for laptops, tablets and phones.

- The plan must be bought within 30 days of delivery.
- Each accidental damage claim has a $49 fee.
- You can make up to 2 accidental damage claims during the plan.

## What the warranty does not cover

- Cosmetic damage, such as scratches, that does not affect how the product works
- Loss or theft
- Damage caused by repairs done by anyone other than the manufacturer or VoltCart
- Normal battery wear over time

## How to make a warranty claim

For a VoltCart warranty or Protection Plan claim, go to **Account > Protection Plans** and choose **Start a claim**. We send you a prepaid shipping box. Repairs or replacements are completed within 10 business days of receiving the product.
