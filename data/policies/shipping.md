# VoltCart Shipping Policy

## Where we ship

VoltCart ships to addresses in the United States and Canada. We do not ship to other countries. We cannot deliver to PO boxes for items that weigh more than 20 kg, such as large TVs.

## Shipping options and costs

- **Standard shipping:** 5–7 business days. Free on orders over $50, otherwise $5.99.
- **Express shipping:** 2–3 business days for $14.99.
- **Next-day shipping:** $29.99. Available in the United States only, for orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day.

## Processing time

Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern Time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day. Other orders leave on the next business day. Pre-orders ship on the product's release date.

## Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a tracking number. You can also track every order under **Account > Orders**.

## Delayed or lost packages

If your tracking has not updated for 7 business days, or your package has not arrived 5 business days after the expected delivery date, contact VoltCart support. We will investigate with the carrier and either send a replacement or give you a full refund.

## Shipping to Canada

Delivery to Canada takes 7–10 business days with standard shipping. Import duties and taxes are calculated and paid at checkout, so there are no extra charges on delivery.
